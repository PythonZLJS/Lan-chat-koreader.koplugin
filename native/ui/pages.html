Uninstall Neighbour Chat?

This stops hosting, unregisters the app, and removes its program files and two launchers.

Your messages, accounts and files are kept. They will be kept inside NeighbourChat-Backups / Uninstalled in Internal Storage. Your other backups stay in the same NeighbourChat-Backups folder.

Cancel
{{end}} {{define "uninstalling"}}

Removing the app...

The app will stop and unregister itself in a few seconds.

Your chat data is kept in a dated folder inside Internal Storage / NeighbourChat-Backups / Uninstalled. Program files and launcher scripts are removed automatically. Copy that backup to your PC if you want to keep it.

If removal does not complete, check NeighbourChat.log in Internal Storage.
